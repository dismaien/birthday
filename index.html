the idea

rainy autumn forest vibes, a hot tub and a sauna, dancing and music, and a cook. you fly in from wherever you are and stay for all of it or part of it.

there are a ton of instruments, so we can jam and sing. there's a projector for karaoke or movies. i won't be making a program. it's co-created, and any and all additions or contributions are welcome. let's make it a cozy, fun time together.

i'd love to have you here.

the place

9 hectares of forest near porto, with a lake, two springs, and mossy boulders everywhere.

the cork room

our warm living room. cork floor, cushions, tea. this is where we'll hang out.

the salon

the big hall, full of instruments, looking out into the trees.

what it costs

nothing. your bed and your meals are my gift to you. you just get yourself here.

if you're very comfortable and want to help with costs, i'd welcome that. but please come either way.

staying past monday is welcome too. those extra nights are on you.

getting there

fly into porto (OPO). a taxi from the airport to the garden is about €45, so it's worth sharing one. once you say yes, i'll send the exact address and match people up by arrival time.

bring

the garden is normally closed in winter, and the big halls are cold and hard to heat. so we'll hang out in the cork room, the small warm one usually used for yoga and meditation. the rest of the warmth is in the sauna and the hot tub. dress for the cold in between.

  • wool socks and slippers
  • a big sweater, layers
  • rain jacket, boots
  • a robe (sauna and hot tub are nude, swimwear if you'd rather)
  • a hot water bottle
  • music, an instrument
