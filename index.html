a forest birthday

come celebrate my birthday with me at the garden. come for a day, or a week, or however long you want.

where

the idea

rainy autumn forest vibes, a hot tub and a sauna, dancing and music, and maybe even a cook. you fly in from wherever you are and stay for all of it or part of it.

i want loved ones around me who actually show up. that's you.

our warm living room. cork floor, cushions, tea. this is where we'll hang out.

money

you get yourself here. the stay will be free or low cost, and i'm still working that out with the garden. i don't want this to be a drain on anyone, so if it's not free it'll go by what you can afford.

if you want to stay past monday, you're welcome to, you just cover those nights yourself.

getting there

fly into porto (OPO). a taxi from the airport to the garden is about €45, so it's worth sharing one. once you say yes, i'll send the exact address and match people up by arrival time.

bring

the garden is normally closed in winter, and the big halls are cold and hard to heat. so we'll hang out in the cork room, the small warm one usually used for yoga and meditation. the rest of the warmth is in the sauna and the hot tub. dress for the cold in between.

  • wool socks and slippers
  • a big sweater, layers
  • rain jacket, boots
  • a robe (sauna and hot tub are nude, swimwear if you'd rather)
  • a hot water bottle
  • music, an instrument
